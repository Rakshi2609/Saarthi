# TVS Credit — Gold Loan FAQ

## How much can I borrow against my gold?
Up to 75% of the gold's market value, based on the day's rate.
Minimum purity: 18 carat. Maximum loan: ₹50 lakh per applicant.

## What is the interest rate?
10.0% – 14.0% p.a. (reducing balance). Lower rates for shorter tenure
and higher purity gold.

## Tenure options
3, 6, 9, 12 months (renewable). Bullet repayment (pay interest monthly,
principal at end) is the most common.

## What documents are required?
1. Aadhaar (mandatory)
2. PAN (mandatory for loans above ₹2 lakh)
3. Passport-size photo
4. Original gold jewellery (for verification + valuation)

## Is CIBIL score required?
No — gold loan is collateral-based, so CIBIL is NOT the primary
decision factor. However, for loans above ₹5 lakh, Saarthi will
still run a fraud check and a credit pull to ensure the customer
isn't over-leveraged across multiple gold loans.

## What happens if I don't pay?
After 90 days of default, the gold is auctioned per RBI Master
Direction on auction of pledged gold (2023). Customer receives
a 30-day prior notice via SMS and registered post.

## Part-release / partial repayment
Available after 3 months. Pay 25% of principal to release 25% of
gold by weight (proportional).
