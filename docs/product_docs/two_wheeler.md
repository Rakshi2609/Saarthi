# TVS Credit — Two Wheeler Loan FAQ

## What is the minimum income requirement?
Salaried: ₹12,000/month net take-home. Self-employed: ₹15,000/month average
bank credit in last 6 months. Farmer / gig worker: case-by-case, may require
co-applicant.

## What documents do I need?
1. Aadhaar (mandatory, OTP eKYC available)
2. PAN card
3. Last 3 months bank statement
4. Salary slip (last 2 months) OR ITR (last 1 year, if self-employed)
5. One passport photograph

## What is the interest rate?
12.5% – 19.5% p.a. (reducing balance). Final rate depends on credit score,
employment type, and loan amount.

## What tenure options are available?
12, 18, 24, 30, 36 months.

## Can I prepay the loan?
Yes, after 6 EMIs. Prepayment charge = 2% of outstanding principal
(for floating rate loans, as per RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025).

## How long does approval take?
Under 60 seconds for pre-qualified applicants with valid bureau score
and clean KYC. Manual underwriter review (refer case): 24-48 hours.

## What is the maximum loan amount?
Up to 100% on-road financing for salaried applicants with score ≥ 750.
Up to 85% for scores 700-749. Subject to vehicle valuation.
