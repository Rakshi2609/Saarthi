# TVS Credit — Digital Lending Directions 2025 (Customer-Facing Summary)

## What is the Key Fact Statement (KFS)?
Every digital loan must be accompanied by a Key Fact Statement before
you accept the loan. It includes: APR (all-in cost), tenure, EMI schedule,
total interest payable, all fees, and grievance officer contact. You
must digitally sign this — not just click "I agree".

## Cooling-off period
You can cancel any digital loan within **1 day** (24 hours) of
disbursement, with no penalty and a full refund. After 24 hours,
prepayment charges apply per your loan agreement.

## Data privacy
Your Aadhaar, PAN, and bank data are stored in India only. No data
is shared with third parties for marketing without explicit consent.
You can request deletion of your data after loan closure.

## First Loss Default Guarantee (FLDG)
Some digital loans through Lending Service Providers (LSPs) carry an
FLDG — meaning the LSP absorbs the first 5% of defaults. This is the
RBI's maximum, and only for digital loans. It does NOT affect your
contract with TVS Credit — you still owe the full amount.

## What if I am harassed by recovery agents?
Per RBI 2025 Directions, recovery must be: in person between 8 AM-7 PM
only, with prior notice, in your preferred language. No threats, no
shaming, no calls to relatives or employers. File a complaint at
[1800-XXX-XXXX] or via the Saarthi app — TVS Credit must resolve in
7 days.

## Grievance Redressal
Level 1: TVS Credit grievance officer (7 days)
Level 2: NBFC ombudsman (RBI) — 30 days
Level 3: Consumer court
