# TVS Credit — Tractor Loan FAQ

## What is the maximum loan amount?
Up to ₹12 lakh for new tractors, up to ₹8 lakh for used tractors
(under 5 years old). Loan-to-value capped at 85% for new, 75% for used.

## What tenure options are available?
36, 48, 60, 72 months. Seasonal repayment (1 EMI/year aligned with
harvest) available for farmers.

## What documents are required?
1. Aadhaar + PAN
2. Land record (khatauni / 7-12 extract) — owned OR leased (min 5 yr lease)
3. Crop pattern document (last 2 seasons)
4. Bank statement (last 12 months — to see crop income seasonality)
5. Passport-size photo
6. Quotations from authorised dealer

## What is the interest rate?
12.0% – 18.0% p.a. (reducing balance). Subsidies:
- NABARD's Interest Subvention Scheme (ISS) — 3% on short-term crop loans up to ₹3 lakh
- State-level agri subsidies (Maharashtra, MP, UP — varies)

## Is there a collateral requirement?
For loans up to ₹2 lakh: no collateral (per RBI FIDD circular
Dec 6, 2024, raising collateral-free agri-loan limit from ₹1.6L to ₹2L).
Above ₹2 lakh: tractor hypothecation + land mortgage.

## How is eligibility different from salaried loans?
Saarthi uses satellite NDVI (last 6 months), weather data (rainfall deficit),
and crop pattern to score farmers. Land record and 2-season crop history
matter more than CIBIL — many farmers have thin or no bureau history.

## Repayment
Monthly OR seasonal (1 EMI at harvest). For seasonal: EMI amount = 10-15%
of estimated crop value. Default grace period: 30 days post-harvest.
